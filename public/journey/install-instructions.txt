HOW TO INSTALL THE HABITNU DEMO ON YOUR IPHONE
================================================

1. Open Safari on your iPhone.
2. Go to: <deployed URL>/journey
3. Tap the Share button (square with an up-arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
5. Confirm the name ("Habitnu") and tap "Add" in the top-right.
6. Close Safari.
7. On your home screen, tap the new Habitnu icon.
8. The app launches full-screen with no browser bars.

NOTES:
- Voice narration requires you to tap "Start Sally's day" once so
  iOS Safari knows to permit the speech synthesis (iOS gestural gate).
- If Nu's voice doesn't play, tap the "Unmute Nu" badge in the
  bottom-right corner once.
- Rotate to portrait for the intended layout.
